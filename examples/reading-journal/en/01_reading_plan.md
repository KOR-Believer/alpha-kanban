# Reading journal plan

A fictional project used in the introduction and README. Statuses and work records illustrate the workflow; they are not real implementation results.

## Goal

Keep a record of books and find them by title. Review and implement one small feature at a time.

## Features

- Record title, author, and reading status.
- Combine title search with a read-books filter.
- Edit book records and confirm before deleting them.
- Sort by title or most recently recorded.

## Agreed scope

Keep filters for the current visit. Searching the text of reading notes is outside this scope. On small screens, place filters below the search field.
